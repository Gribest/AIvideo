LAGO DI BRAIES
Instagram vs Reality
Here's our honest family guide.
Lago di Braies
South Tyrol, Italy · 1,496 m
Lago di Braies sits at
almost 1,500 metres in the
Dolomites of South Tyrol.
We arrived with our little one,
a camera,
and a bag full of snacks.
PARKING

P3 / P2 · cheaper
short walk to the lake
Parking tip:
P4 is closest but pricey.
P3 or P2 save you money.
First,
a snack stop for the
smallest member of the crew.
Then you round the corner,
and there it is.
Seekofel
2,810 m above the lake
Above the lake rises the Seekofel,
almost 2,800 metres high.
Every few minutes,
the light shifts across that
rock face.
The water is so clear
you can count the pebbles.
In autumn,
the larches turn gold.
Green, gold,
and that turquoise water.
It hardly looks real.
And when the sun broke
through at the sandy end,
the colours changed completely.
Our daughter headed straight for
the sand.
Plenty of pebble beach for
little legs.
She could have stayed here
all day.
ROWBOAT

pay at the boathouse
life jackets for kids
The famous wooden rowboats:
€55 for 40 minutes.
Little one on the bench...
Captain Dad
...and Dad on the oars.
From inside the boat,
it's forty minutes of pure calm.
The only sounds are the oars,
and the odd happy squeal.
From the shore,
you are the postcard.

Now,
the honest part.
INSTAGRAM
REALITY
Most photos online are shot
at sunrise and edited.
On a normal day it's greyer,
flatter,
and busier.
By midday the beach is packed,
and there are boats in
every shot.
Everyone is chasing the same photo,
from the same spot.
Autumn = low water
In autumn the water drops,
and the shore turns to gravel.
And with a toddler?
Forty minutes was exactly enough.
Bring snacks,
a warm layer,
and a toy for the boat.
TIP 1
Go at sunset
or after the boats close
= empty lake, no crowds
Want the Instagram shot?
Come at sunset,
or after the boats close,
when the lake goes quiet.
Golden light,
still water,
and the boats lined up
at the dock.

Plan 2 to 3 hours
Plan two to three hours:
the boat,
the loop walk round the lake,
and a snack.

Skip P4,
park at P3 or P2,
and in summer check whether

So,
Instagram or reality? Honestly... both.
It's busy,
and it's not quite the postcard.
But in that boat,
with our daughter,
under those mountains,
we didn't mind at all.
