## Description
That famous turquoise lake with the wooden boats and not another soul around? Here's what Lago di Braies (Pragser Wildsee) in the Italian Dolomites is *really* like, from a family day with our little one.

We cover parking (P4 vs P3/P2), the €55 rowboat (40 minutes), how long to spend there, the crowds, autumn low water, and the one trick for empty-lake photos: go at sunset or after the boats close.

Verdict: TRIP. Busy, not quite the postcard, and still one of the most beautiful places we've been.
